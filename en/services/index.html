FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of projects do you work on?

We work on AI integrations, process automation, custom web and mobile applications, SaaS products, MVPs and modernisation of existing systems.

How is pricing determined?

Scope, technical uncertainty, timeline and required expertise are clarified after the initial assessment.

Is the first conversation paid?

No. The first conversation and preliminary project assessment are free.

Can you take over an existing project?

Yes. We first review the codebase, infrastructure and open risks, then propose a practical transition plan.

Do you provide support after development?

Yes. We can provide maintenance, monitoring, issue resolution and ongoing development based on the need.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Türkiye is our primary market, and we also work remotely with teams abroad.

Can you build only an MVP?

Yes. We can define a focused MVP that tests the idea’s most important assumptions.
